Abed K. Musaffar – Biography

Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of California, Santa Barbara

Hello! My name is Abed K. Musaffar and I am a doctoral candidate in the Department of Mechanical Engineering and Center for Control, Dynamical Systems, and Computation at the University of California, Santa Barbara (UCSB) where I am advised by Professor Francesco Bullo. My research interests are focused on social networks, with an emphasis on studying decision-making dynamics within human-AI teams. Within my research, I have designed and conducted experiments with real human participants, drawing on techniques from reinforcement learning, natural language processing, dynamical systems, and cognitive psychology to model decision-making behavior and design adversaries who can identify and exploit decision-making vulnerabilities in human-AI interactions. My research has appeared in the International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR) 2026 proceedings, and was awarded a spotlight talk at a workshop in ICLR 2025.

Before my Ph.D., I completed a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering at UCSB. During my undergraduate, I held internship positions at NASA Ames Research Center and Venturi Astrolab, and conducted undergraduate research under Professor Samantha Daly. For my senior capstone at UCSB, I led a team of students to design a lunar regolith excavator which was tested on Venturi Astrolab’s FLEX Rover. Under my leadership, the team was awarded UC Santa Barbara’s prestigious award for Most Outstanding Technical Design in Mechanical Engineering, with the details of our design published in the Space Resources Roundtable XXII. During my undergraduate degree, I was also awarded the Zed Factor Fellowship, Forbes’ Science Award 2020’s “Most Interesting Newcomer” whose award I used to sponsor an initiative called “Space for All” to connect students from underrepresented minorities with opportunities in aerospace.
