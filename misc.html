Abed K. Musaffar – Miscellanea

Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of California, Santa Barbara

Outside of my research, I have several hobbies.

Gaming: I have a 2000+ total level OSRS ironman.

Cars: I drive a modified 2005 RSX Type-S and a 2000 4Runner 4x4. My favorite cars are any 1993+ Toyota MR2 Turbo (last turbo revision sold in the US) and any Porsche 911 Reimagined by Singer. One of my dreams is to one day have my own garage so I can tackle bigger automotive projects without inconveniencing anyone around me.

Foraging: My keen interest in the natural world is self-reinforcing through my culinary curiosity. As a result, I enjoy learning what plants around me are edible and trying to cook with them. My favorite plants that I have eaten include:

  • Chicken of the woods: an edible mushroom which definitely has the same texture as chicken

  • Wild common mallow: this is a weed that grows everywhere and can be stir-fried

  • Gundelia/Tumble thistle: my favorite plant which is said to taste like a combination of artichoke/asparagus

Exercise: I am a (former) competitive swimmer and an avid runner. My favorite thing to do is extreme workouts. My proudest accomplishment is running 25 miles in my 3rd grade walkathon to win an XBOX 360 in a bet with my dad.

Reading: I am particularly interested in philosophy because it keeps my mind busy. Some of my favorite books which I have read recently include:

  • Notes from Underground – Fyodor Dostoevsky

  • The Prophet – Khalil Gibran

  • The Myth of Sisyphus – Albert Camus

Pets: I have three cats whom I love: Cookie (the most responsible), Toast (the least responsible), and Pigeon (the loudest).

Other hobbies I engage with on a regular basis include:

  • Journaling

  • Art collecting

  • Audio listening experience

  • Finance (market microstructure, order book modeling, forecasting, etc.)
